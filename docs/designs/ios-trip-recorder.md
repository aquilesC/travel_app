## Problem Statement

On the road the journal is fed by WhatsApp (Twilio): low-resolution travel copies, no GPS track, no
live view for family, and full-resolution originals added by hand afterwards from a laptop. GPX
tracks need a separate logger app. The website already turns photos + GPX into grouped draft entries
(`trips/importing.py`), but only when someone uploads them from a computer.

## What Makes This Cool

- **Family follows along live.** The phone logs position at an interval you choose and ships it in
  batches, so the share page shows where you are and the path so far, not just yesterday's digest.
- **Picking the day's photos is quick.** The app shows only the photos from a given day, time window
  or place on the map. You pick the good ones and upload those originals. The phone's own position
  log places them on the map and groups them into entries.
- **One app for posting on the road.** Text, voice notes, photos and video go straight to the trip,
  offline-first, without WhatsApp's size limits or a Twilio account.

## Constraints

- Two users (Aquiles and wife), both on iPhone. Not a product; no App Store listing.
- Sri Lanka (2026-12-21) is out of scope: that trip runs on WhatsApp + web as planned.
- Self-hosted Django behind Cloudflare free plan: 100 MB max request body, so large uploads must be
  chunked (the existing `media.py` chunked-upload protocol already handles ≤ 50 MB chunks).
- Mobile data abroad is expensive/patchy: position batches are tiny and can go over cellular. Media
  uploads are resumable and the user chooses whether they need wifi.
- iOS does not run apps on a timer in the background. Location sampling and batch uploads happen
  when iOS delivers location updates to the app, not on an exact clock.

## Premises (agreed)

1. Sri Lanka runs on WhatsApp + web; the app's first real test is a weekend trip in early 2027.
2. The app records position, lets you choose and upload photos, and posts text, audio, photos and video.
   Longer editing and cleanup happen later on the web.
3. Grouping and drafting stay server-side, reusing `importing.py` (`stage_file`, `fill_locations`,
   `group_points`, `create_entries`) and the import review page. One source of truth; rule changes
   need no app update.
4. Distribution is TestFlight / Xcode installs for the two of you only. Needs an Apple Developer
   account ($99/yr). No App Review.
5. Step one is server-side: a small versioned JSON API with token auth. This narrowly reverses the
   v1 epic's "no API in v1" decision.
6. Each phone works on its own. Its photos and positions go up under that user. Nothing syncs between
   the two devices, and the server does no merging. Cleaning up duplicates and overlaps is a later web task.
7. In-app posting makes Twilio optional. WhatsApp ingest stays in the codebase and keeps working.
   Whether to drop it is decided after the app has carried a real trip.

## Approaches Considered

- **A: Shortcuts + off-the-shelf GPX logger.** Kept as a throwaway probe of the API only. Can't do
  live position or in-app posting.
- **B: Native SwiftUI app.** Chosen.
- **C: Expo / React Native.** Rejected: the hard parts (background location, background uploads,
  PhotoKit) are exactly where cross-platform layers leak; Android is not needed.
- PWA rejected: no background GPS, no photo-library access.

## Recommended Approach: native SwiftUI app

### Location recording and live sharing

- **Interval set by the user**, e.g. 30 s / 1 min / 5 min / 15 min, plus Off. The app runs CoreLocation
  continuously at reduced accuracy (`distanceFilter` set from the interval) and **keeps one point per
  interval**. Short intervals use more battery; the settings screen says so.
- Points are stored locally first (SwiftData), so nothing is lost offline.
- **Upload interval set by the user** (e.g. every 5 / 15 / 60 min). When a location update wakes the
  app and the last upload was longer ago than that, it posts all unsent points in one small request.
  Without signal, points pile up and go in the next batch. iOS keeps the background app alive while
  location is on, so this needs no special background-task scheduling.
- Server keeps the points in a **new `Position` model** (`trip`, `user`, `recorded_at`, `lat`, `lon`,
  `accuracy_m`, `received_at`; unique on `(user, recorded_at)` so retried batches are idempotent).
  The trip map draws them as a live path per traveller, next to uploaded GPX `Track`s.
- For drafting, the server turns a user's positions for a day into the same point series that
  `fill_locations` already reads from GPX (`track_series`), so photos without GPS get placed from
  the phone's own log.
- **Live view for family:** the share page (`/s/<token>/`) shows the latest position and today's
  path, refreshed by polling (no websockets). Visibility is a per-trip setting (see Open Questions).

### Photo selection (not bulk upload)

- Photo picker built on PhotoKit with **filters by date range / time window** (default: today, in the
  trip's time zone) and **by location** (map view of the matching photos; draw or pan to an area).
- The user selects, then the app uploads **originals** (HEIC/video untouched) through the chunked
  protocol into an import batch for the trip. The server groups them into draft entries as today.
- Dedupe by `sha256` (already indexed), so a photo picked twice is uploaded once.
- Needs full photo-library read access (the limited picker can't filter by location).

### In-app posting (WhatsApp replacement)

- Compose screen: text (markdown), photo/video (camera or library), **voice note** (record in app).
- Creates an `Entry` with `source="app"`, the current position and time attached, `is_public`
  toggle defaulting like the web editor.
- Offline queue: posts are saved locally and sent when there's signal; media goes through the
  same chunked, resumable upload. Large video waits for wifi unless the user overrides.
- Server reuses the media processing pipeline already used by the web and WhatsApp (HEIC, ffmpeg
  720p + poster, audio).

### Server API (`api/v1/`)

- Auth: allauth headless (app client, session token) on top of the existing invite-only email login.
- `GET trips/`: member trips (reuse `access.member_trips`) with the current one marked.
- `POST trips/<id>/positions/`: batch of points; returns the accepted count.
- `GET s/<token>/live.json`: latest position + today's path for the share page (respects visibility).
- Chunked upload endpoints: reuse the protocol in `media.py`.
- `POST trips/<id>/imports/`: selected photos → import batch → returns the review URL.
- `POST trips/<id>/entries/`: in-app post (text + optional media ids + position).
- `Entry.source` gains `"app"`.

### App screens

1. **Trip:** current trip, recording on/off, last sync, queued items.
2. **Post:** text / photo / video / voice.
3. **Photos:** filter by day, time window or map area → select → upload.
4. **Settings:** recording interval, upload interval, wifi-only media, account.

## Decided after review

- **Live location privacy:** members always see it; share-link viewers only when enabled per trip,
  delayed (default 60 min) and rounded (default ~1 km); per-user privacy zones (e.g. home) are never shown.
  Specified in `issues/27-live-location.md`.
- **Website goes API-first and adopts TailwindCSS:** `issues/19-epic-v4-api-tailwind.md`;
  app work in `issues/ios/`.

## Open Questions

- Battery at 30 s / 1 min intervals over a full travel day. Measure on a weekend before choosing a default.
- Do positions stay in their own model forever, or get exported to a GPX `Track` per day? The epic made
  GPX the source of truth for tracks; positions are a different input. Proposed: keep `Position` as
  the source, offer GPX export.
- iCloud "Optimize iPhone Storage": originals may need downloading from iCloud first
  (`isNetworkAccessAllowed`), which costs data abroad.
- Should the evening "add words" notification stay? It's less central now that photos are picked by hand.

## Success Criteria

- A full weekend trip in early 2027 recorded with the app and **no laptop upload and no WhatsApp**:
  - live path visible to family during the trip
  - chosen photos at full resolution
  - text, voice and video posts arriving from both phones
- Battery at end of day ≥ 20% at the chosen default interval.
- No lost or duplicated positions or media after flaky connectivity (idempotent batches + sha256).

## Distribution Plan

The server ships through the existing `git pull && docker compose up -d --build`. App CI is
deferred; manual archives are fine for two users.

## What I noticed about how you think

- "Cleaning up can be done at a later moment": you cut device sync and merging from the scope instead of
  designing them. That's the most expensive problem in the plan, gone.
- "The app should not upload all photos": you'd rather choose what goes into the journal than collect
  everything. The photo filters exist to make choosing quick.
- "I'll keep the options open for the time being" on Twilio: you'll replace the working path only after the new one has carried a trip.
